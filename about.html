About Elimupeo Foundation

Elimupeo Foundation is Kenya's #1 Opportunity Hub covering all 47 counties. We verify every opportunity one by one on official .go.ke, company career pages, and trusted international portals.

Our Permanent Mission

Elimupeo Foundation exists to ensure every Kenyan youth, graduate, and jobseeker has free, equal, and transparent access to verified opportunities. We do not charge any fee, we do not ask for M-Pesa before shortlist, and we never sell jobs. All jobs on elimupeo.co.ke are 100% FREE forever to view and apply.

Founded to bridge the gap between opportunity and access, Elimupeo Foundation operates daily with integrity, transparency, and community service. We check each listing directly from the source, remove expired posts to our Closed Archive for accountability, and arrange the latest authentic opportunities at the top for your convenience.

Whether you are seeking government jobs via PSC, TSC teaching vacancies, county jobs, parastatal internships like KPC and KMTC, airport jobs at KAA, marine jobs at KMA and Bandari, banking jobs at KCB, or international NGO jobs at UN, World Vision, and Red Cross, Elimupeo Foundation is your trusted partner.

What We Do

• Daily verification of government, county, private sector, and international jobs
• Free access - no registration fee
• County filter - all 47 counties
• Transparency - closed jobs moved to archive
• Community support via voluntary donations only
