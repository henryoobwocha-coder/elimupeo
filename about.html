About Elimupeo Foundation

Elimupeo Foundation is operated by Sir Henry, based in Eldoret, Uasin Gishu County, Kenya.

What we do: We verify and publish genuine jobs, scholarships, internships, grants from official sources: Public Service Commission, TSC, County Public Service Boards, KMTC, KISE, KAA, KPA, UN, NGOs, universities.

Free: All listings are free. We never charge via M-Pesa. Official employers never ask for fees.

No guarantee: We do not guarantee scholarship awards or job appointments. Final decision is by official organization.

Verify: Always verify information with official organization via Official Application Link before applying.

Contact: info@elimupeo.co.ke | Eldoret, Kenya
