BOM Teachers To Government Payroll - What Was Announced

All BOM teachers to be moved to government payroll. Plus 20,000 new teachers to be recruited.

The Government of Kenya has announced that all teachers currently employed by Boards of Management (BOM) will be moved to the government payroll. The announcement also included a plan to recruit an additional 20,000 teachers to address the teacher shortage in schools across the country.

This has brought hope to thousands of BOM teachers who have worked for years on low salaries, without job security, medical cover, or pension. In this guide, ElimuPeo explains what it means, who qualifies, and what to do now.

Who Are BOM Teachers?

BOM teachers are teachers employed directly by the Board of Management of a school, not by TSC. Schools hire BOM teachers when TSC has not posted enough teachers. The school pays the BOM teacher from school funds. In many rural schools and Junior Schools, BOM teachers are the ones keeping learning going. They teach full timetables, prepare learners for exams, and take part in co-curricular activities, just like TSC teachers. The challenge has always been pay and security.

What Was Announced? Two Parts

Part 1: BOM Teachers To Government Payroll
All BOM teachers in public schools will be absorbed by TSC and put on government payroll. They will get a TSC Number, monthly salary, and pension.

Part 2: 20,000 New Teachers To Be Recruited
In addition, the government will recruit 20,000 new teachers. These are for unemployed trained teachers who are not currently working as BOM teachers.

Do not mix the two. If you are a BOM teacher, you fall under Part 1. If you are unemployed and not working in any school, you fall under Part 2.

Who Qualifies?

1. You must be a Kenyan citizen.
2. You must be a trained teacher with P1, Diploma, or Bachelor of Education.
3. You must be registered with TSC and have a valid TSC Number.
4. You must be currently working as a BOM teacher in a registered public primary, junior, or secondary school.
5. You must have proof of BOM employment - appointment letter, contract, or payslips.

Teachers in private schools are not BOM teachers. Teachers with fake certificates or who are not registered with TSC will not qualify.

Documents To Prepare Now

Do not wait. Prepare a folder with: National ID, TSC Registration Certificate, KCSE Certificate, P1 / Diploma / Degree Certificate and Transcripts, BOM Appointment Letter, BOM Contract, Recent Payslips if available, and Letter from Headteacher confirming you are currently teaching. Keep originals. Never give originals to anyone.

What Will The Process Look Like?

Step 1: TSC will publish an official circular on www.tsc.go.ke
Step 2: Headteachers and County Directors will submit lists of BOM teachers in their schools.
Step 3: TSC county offices will verify documents. You will be called for physical verification.
Step 4: Successful teachers will get appointment letters and be posted.

Until TSC releases the circular, any list, form, or WhatsApp link claiming to be the official BOM form is fake.

How Much Will BOM Teachers Earn?

Salary depends on qualification. Primary BOM teachers will be placed in Job Group B5. Diploma teachers in Junior and Secondary will be placed in Job Group C1. Graduate teachers will be placed in Job Group C2. You will also get medical cover and pension, which most BOM teachers do not have now. Exact amount will be in your appointment letter.

Scam Warning

Fraudsters are already using this announcement. Fake TSC WhatsApp Groups asking for Ksh 500. Fake M-Pesa calls asking for processing fee. Fake Google Forms asking for ID and M-Pesa PIN. TSC recruitment is completely FREE. If anyone asks for money, it is a scam. Report to TSC or police.

What Should BOM Teachers Do Today?

1. Continue teaching and keep a good record.
2. Make sure your school has your correct TSC Number, ID, and phone.
3. Prepare your document folder.
4. Check only official sources: www.tsc.go.ke, Citizen TV, NTV, KBC, and ElimuPeo.
5. Do not pay anyone. Do not join fake groups.
