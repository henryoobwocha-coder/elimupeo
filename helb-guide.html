HELB Guide - How To Apply, Loan Types And Repayment

Full guide on Higher Education Loans Board (HELB) in Kenya. How to apply, who qualifies, and how to avoid fake HELB messages.

HELB gives loans and bursaries to Kenyan students in universities, TVET colleges, and other higher learning institutions. For many students from Kisii, Nyamira, Kisumu, Homa Bay and Migori, HELB is the only way to pay school fees, accommodation, and upkeep.

In this guide, ElimuPeo explains the types of HELB loans, who qualifies, how to apply step by step, and how to avoid scammers.

Types Of HELB Loans

1. Undergraduate Loan: For students joining university for the first time through KUCCPS.
2. TVET Loan: For students in Technical and Vocational Colleges.
3. Subsequent Application: For students in Year 2, 3, 4 who already got HELB in Year 1.
4. Jielimishe Loan: For salaried Kenyans who want to further their studies.

Who Qualifies?

1. Must be a Kenyan citizen.
2. Must have a National ID.
3. Must have been admitted to a recognized university or TVET college in Kenya.
4. Must have KCSE Certificate.
5. Must have parents or guardians with ID and KRA PIN.

Documents You Need

Your National ID, KCSE Certificate, Admission Letter from university or college, Parents National IDs, Parents KRA PIN, and 2 guarantors with IDs and KRA PIN. Your guarantors must be employed Kenyans who know you.

How To Apply - Step By Step

Step 1: Go to www.helb.co.ke
Step 2: Click on Student Portal and create an account.
Step 3: Fill your personal details, education details, and family details.
Step 4: Fill your guarantors details.
Step 5: Submit and print the form. Take it to your Chief, your religious leader, and your institution for stamping.
Step 6: Scan and upload the stamped form back to the portal.

Application is FREE on the portal. HELB does not charge to create an account.

How Much Will You Get?

HELB amount depends on your family income and your course. Students from poor families get more. The loan is paid in two parts - tuition to the school and upkeep to your M-Pesa or bank. You will get an SMS when money is sent. Check your portal to confirm.

Common Mistakes

1. Using wrong KRA PIN for parents or guarantors.
2. Not uploading the stamped form.
3. Applying late when the portal is slow.
4. Giving wrong phone number - HELB uses SMS to communicate.
5. Not updating your details when you change school or course.

How To Repay HELB

You start repaying 1 year after you finish school. You can repay through M-Pesa Paybill, bank, or through your employer. If you do not repay, HELB will list you with CRB and you will not get a clearance certificate. Pay small amounts even when you are jobless to keep your record clean.

Scam Warning

HELB does not send loans through WhatsApp links. Fake messages like "Congratulations, you have won HELB Ksh 50,000, click this link and enter M-Pesa PIN" are scams. HELB never asks for M-Pesa PIN. Apply only on www.helb.co.ke
