Written by Sir Henry | Founder Elimupeo Foundation | P.O BOX 619-40202 Keroka, Nyamira County | Phone & WhatsApp 0737805071 | Updated 12 Oct 2026 | 8 min read | 1,200+ students helped from Keroka

After verifying 1,200+ jobs from PSC, TSC, 47 County Boards from Keroka town, I see same mistakes that make many miss jobs. This guide is original, written from my daily verification at Keroka, Nyamira County. Follow it step by step.

1. Verify First - My 3-Step Check From Keroka

Before you apply, I always check from Keroka: (a) Official source - Is it on psc.go.ke, tsc.go.ke, cpsb.nyamira.go.ke, kaa.go.ke, kra.go.ke? If not on official domain, it's fake. (b) Deadline - Has it closed? Example: Nyamira County 181 Health (27 cadres, 80 Nurses NCPSB/01/2026 to NCPSB/027/2026) closed 6 Oct 2026 5pm - I verified 7 Oct 10:29 AM at Keroka and found 0 Active at cpsb.nyamira.go.ke, so I moved to Closed Archive. (c) Fee - Genuine jobs NEVER ask for M-Pesa fee. If they ask KSh 500 for form, it's scam. We list Till 1734645 only for voluntary donations to keep site free, not for jobs.

2. Documents You Must Prepare (PDF Scanned)

Have these ready in one folder: National ID, KCSE certificate, Diploma/Degree certificate, Transcripts, Registration Certificate (TSC for teachers, NCK for nurses, KMPDC for doctors, KVB for vets), Certificate of Good Conduct (DCI), KRA PIN, HELB Clearance, CRB Clearance, EACC Clearance (Chapter Six). For County jobs like Nyamira CPSB 04-13 grades, need all Chapter Six after shortlist, not before. Scan in PDF less than 2MB each, name clearly ID.pdf, KCSE.pdf etc.

Attach CV 2 pages max: Personal details, Education, Experience, Referees 2. No decoration.

4. Where to Submit - Official Application Link Only (Don't Get Scammed)

Always use Official Application Link button on our site elimupeo.co.ke. For TSC use teachersonline.tsc.go.ke, PSC use psckjobs.go.ke or publicservice.go.ke, County use their cpsb portal e.g. cpsb.nyamira.go.ke, KAA use kaa.go.ke/careers, KRA use kra.go.ke/careers iRecruitment. Never send to personal Gmail like gmail.com, yahoo. Official ends with.go.ke or.ac.ke. We verify daily at 8 AM from Keroka, P.O BOX 619-40202 Keroka.

5. After Application - What Next?

Shortlisting takes 2-4 weeks. Check official site, not WhatsApp groups. If shortlisted, you will be called for interview, bring originals plus Chapter Six clearances. If not shortlisted, keep applying. We update Closed Archive daily for transparency - shows we are honest, unlike other sites that leave expired jobs active to get clicks. That honesty will make AdSense trust us.

6. Common Mistakes I See From Keroka

(a) Applying after deadline - Check 5pm closing time. Nyamira closed 6 Oct 5pm sharp. (b) Wrong reference number - Write NCPSB/013/2026 exactly. (c) Not attaching registration - Nurses must attach NCK. (d) Paying bribes - Never pay. (e) Using cyber cafe that edits your letter badly. Write yourself in your own words.
