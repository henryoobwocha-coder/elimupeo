How We Verify Jobs In Kenya

Why you can trust ElimuPeo. Our 5-step verification process before any job is published.

Every day, Kenyans lose money to fake jobs, fake loans, and fake scholarships on WhatsApp and Facebook. ElimuPeo was started to stop this. We check every opportunity from the official source before we publish it.

Here is exactly how we verify.

Step 1: Check The Official Source

We never copy from WhatsApp or Facebook. We check the official website first: www.tsc.go.ke for teachers, www.mod.go.ke for KDF, www.npsc.go.ke for police, www.helb.co.ke for HELB, www.psc.go.ke for government jobs. If it is not on the official website, we do not publish it.

Step 2: Check Mainstream Media

We cross-check with Citizen TV, NTV, KBC, and Daily Nation. If a big job is real, it will be on Citizen TV or NTV. If it is only on one WhatsApp group, it is fake.

Step 3: Check The Details

We check 5 things:
1. Is the application FREE? Real government jobs are free.
2. Is there an official email ending in .go.ke? Fake jobs use Gmail.
3. Is there a physical office you can visit?
4. Does it ask for M-Pesa PIN or OTP? If yes, it is fake.
5. Is the deadline realistic? Fake jobs say "Apply in 2 hours."

Step 4: Call Or Visit

When possible, we call the county office or visit the institution to confirm. For example, for TSC jobs, we confirm with TSC County Office in Kisii, Nyamira, Kisumu, and Homa Bay.

Step 5: Publish With Warning

Only after all 4 steps, we publish on ElimuPeo. We add a scam warning to every article, and we add a link to the official source so you can verify yourself.

What We NEVER Publish

We never publish jobs that ask for money, jobs that ask for M-Pesa PIN, jobs that are only on WhatsApp, or jobs with no official source. If you see such a job on ElimuPeo, report it to us immediately.

How You Can Verify Yourself

Before you apply for any job, ask these 3 questions:
1. Is it on the official .go.ke website?
2. Is it on Citizen TV or NTV?
3. Does it ask for money? If yes, it is fake.
