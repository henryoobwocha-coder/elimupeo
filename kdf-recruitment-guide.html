KDF Recruitment - How To Apply And Avoid Fake Calls

Full guide on Kenya Defence Forces recruitment, requirements, centres and how to spot fake messages.

Kenya Defence Forces (KDF) recruitment is one of the most sought-after opportunities for young Kenyans. Every year, thousands of young men and women turn up at recruitment centres in Kisii, Kisumu, Nyamira, Homa Bay, Migori, and across all counties, hoping to join the Army, Navy, or Air Force.

In this guide, ElimuPeo explains who qualifies, what documents you need, how recruitment works, what happens at the centre, and how to avoid fraudsters who steal money from applicants.

Who Can Apply For KDF?

General Service Officer Cadets: Must have KCSE mean grade B (Plain) and above, with C+ in English, Mathematics and one Science. Age 18 to 26 years. Must be a Kenyan citizen with no criminal record.

Servicemen / Servicewomen - General Recruits: Must have KCSE mean grade D (Plain) and above. Age 18 to 26 years. Must be a Kenyan citizen, physically fit, and with no criminal record.

Specialists - Medical, Engineering, IT, Music, etc: Must have a relevant Diploma or Degree, plus KCSE. Age up to 30 years depending on the trade.

You must be medically fit, with correct height and weight. Minimum height for men is 5 feet 3 inches. Minimum height for women is 5 feet 2 inches. You must not have tattoos, body piercings, or scars from criminal activity.

Documents You Must Carry

Carry originals and copies of: National ID Card, KCSE Certificate, Birth Certificate, Academic Certificates for specialists, and a letter from your Chief or Assistant Chief confirming you are from that location. Do not laminate your certificates. Do not give your original ID to anyone at the gate.

How KDF Recruitment Works - Step By Step

Step 1: Announcement
KDF announces recruitment in newspapers, on www.mod.go.ke, and on KBC, Citizen TV, NTV. ElimuPeo will also post it. The announcement lists the dates and centres per county.

Step 2: Go To Your Centre
Go to the recruitment centre in your county on the exact date given. Arrive early, by 6am. Carry your documents and wear sports clothes and sports shoes. Do not pay anyone to get a queue number.

Step 3: Physical Screening
Officers check your height, weight, eyesight, hearing, teeth, and general fitness. You will run, do push-ups and sit-ups. If you fail, you are sent home immediately.

Step 4: Document Verification
Officers check your ID, KCSE, and Birth Certificate. Fake documents are detected immediately. You will be handed to police if you present fake documents.

Step 5: Medical Examination
If you pass physical and documents, you go for full medical examination at a military hospital. This includes blood test, urine test, and chest check.

Step 6: Final Selection and Training
Successful candidates get a calling letter to report to Recruits Training School in Eldoret. Training is about 6 months.

What To Wear And What To Bring

Wear a plain t-shirt, shorts or track suit, and running shoes. Carry water and a small snack. Carry your documents in a clear folder. Do not carry a big bag, jewellery, or a lot of money. Phones are allowed but will be kept during screening.

Common Mistakes That Make Applicants Fail

1. Coming late to the centre.
2. Carrying fake or laminated certificates.
3. Having tattoos or piercings.
4. Being overweight or unfit - start running and training 2 months before.
5. Trying to bribe an officer - you will be arrested immediately.

Scam Warning - Very Important

KDF recruitment is FREE. No one in KDF will call you to ask for Ksh 5,000, Ksh 10,000, or Ksh 50,000 to put your name on the list. Fake calls like "Hello, this is Major from KDF, send money to this M-Pesa to secure your slot" are scams. KDF does not recruit on WhatsApp. KDF does not send calling letters by WhatsApp. If anyone asks for money, report to the nearest police station and to KDF headquarters.

What To Do Now To Prepare

1. Train every morning - run 3km, do push-ups and sit-ups.
2. Eat well and avoid alcohol and smoking.
3. Keep your documents ready in a folder.
4. Follow only official sources: www.mod.go.ke, KBC, Citizen TV, and ElimuPeo.
5. Never pay anyone.
