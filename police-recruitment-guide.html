Police Recruitment Guide - How To Apply In Kenya

Full guide on National Police Service recruitment, requirements, centres, training and scams to avoid.

The National Police Service (NPS) recruits young Kenyans every year to join the Kenya Police, Administration Police, and Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI). Many young people in Kisii, Nyamira, Kisumu, Homa Bay and Migori dream of wearing the uniform and serving the country.

In this guide, ElimuPeo explains who qualifies, what documents to carry, how recruitment works, and how to avoid fraudsters.

Who Can Apply?

Age: 18 to 28 years old.
Education: KCSE mean grade D+ (Plus) and above, with D+ in English and Kiswahili.
Citizenship: Must be a Kenyan citizen with a valid National ID.
Height: Men minimum 5 feet 8 inches. Women minimum 5 feet 3 inches.
Other: Must be physically fit, with no criminal record, no tattoos, and no history of drug abuse.

Documents You Must Carry

Carry originals and copies of: National ID, KCSE Certificate, Birth Certificate, Academic Certificates, and a letter from your Chief confirming you are from that location. Do not laminate your certificates. Do not give your original ID to anyone.

How Police Recruitment Works

Step 1: Announcement
NPS announces recruitment on www.npsc.go.ke, in newspapers, and on Citizen TV, NTV, KBC. ElimuPeo will also post it. The announcement lists dates and centres per county.

Step 2: Go To Your Centre
Go to the centre in your county on the exact date. Arrive by 6am. Wear sports clothes and sports shoes. Carry your documents in a clear folder.

Step 3: Physical Screening
Officers check height, weight, eyesight, hearing, and fitness. You will run, do push-ups and sit-ups. If you fail, you go home.

Step 4: Document Verification
Officers check your ID, KCSE, and Birth Certificate. Fake documents lead to immediate arrest.

Step 5: Medical And Interview
If you pass, you go for medical examination and a short interview about why you want to join the police.

Step 6: Training
Successful candidates report to Kiganjo Police Training College for 9 months training.

How To Prepare

1. Train every morning - run 3km, push-ups, sit-ups.
2. Eat well, avoid alcohol and smoking.
3. Practice answering: Why do you want to join the police? Speak clearly and honestly.
4. Keep your documents ready.

Common Mistakes

1. Coming late.
2. Carrying fake certificates.
3. Having tattoos.
4. Being unfit.
5. Trying to bribe an officer - you will be arrested.

Scam Warning

Police recruitment is FREE. No officer will call you to ask for Ksh 10,000 or Ksh 20,000 to secure a slot. Fake calls like "This is Inspector from NPS, send money to this M-Pesa" are scams. NPS does not recruit on WhatsApp. If anyone asks for money, report to the nearest police station.
