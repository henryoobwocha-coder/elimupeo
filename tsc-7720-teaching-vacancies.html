TSC 7,720 Teaching Vacancies - Replacement P&P

5,609 Primary + 2,086 Secondary + 25 Junior = 7,720. Permanent and Pensionable.

The Teachers Service Commission (TSC) has announced 7,720 replacement teaching vacancies for Primary, Secondary and Junior Schools across Kenya. These are Permanent and Pensionable (P&P) posts. This is good news for trained and registered teachers who are currently unemployed or working as BOM teachers.

In this guide, ElimuPeo explains the breakdown, who qualifies, what documents you need, how to apply step by step, and how to avoid scams.

Breakdown of the Vacancies

1. Primary Schools: 5,609 vacancies

2. Secondary Schools: 2,086 vacancies

3. Junior Schools: 25 vacancies

Total: 7,720 vacancies

These are replacement vacancies. That means TSC is replacing teachers who have left service through retirement, resignation, death, or transfer. You will be posted to a school with a real vacancy, not to a waiting list.

Who Qualifies?

To qualify, you must meet all of these:

1. You must be a Kenyan citizen.
2. You must be a trained teacher with P1 Certificate, Diploma, or Bachelor of Education.
3. You must be registered with TSC and have a valid TSC Number.
4. You must not be currently employed by TSC on permanent terms.
5. You must meet the minimum KCSE grade for your level.

Documents You Need

Prepare originals and clear copies of: National ID, TSC Certificate, KCSE Certificate, P1 / Diploma / Degree Certificate and Transcripts, and your TSC Number. Keep your originals. Never give them to a broker.

How To Apply - Step By Step

Step 1: Go to www.tsc.go.ke
Step 2: Click on Careers / Online Services
Step 3: Login with your TSC Number and password
Step 4: Select the vacancy for your county and level
Step 5: Fill the form correctly and submit. Take a screenshot of your confirmation.

Application is FREE. TSC does not charge any fee to apply.

How TSC Selects Teachers

TSC uses merit. Priority is given to teachers who graduated earlier, teachers who have stayed longer without employment, and teachers applying in their home county. Having a higher qualification also helps. No one can buy a slot. If someone asks for money to put your name on the list, it is a scam.

Common Mistakes To Avoid

1. Applying for the wrong level.
2. Using a wrong TSC Number or expired password.
3. Uploading blurred documents.
4. Applying for many counties at once.
5. Waiting until the last minute when the portal is slow.

Scam Warning

Never pay anyone. TSC does not ask for M-Pesa, OTP, or PIN. TSC does not recruit on WhatsApp. Do not join WhatsApp groups asking for Ksh 500 registration. Apply only on www.tsc.go.ke

What To Do After Applying

After applying, check your TSC portal regularly. TSC will publish merit lists per county. If you are shortlisted, you will be called for document verification at the County Office. Carry all your original documents.
